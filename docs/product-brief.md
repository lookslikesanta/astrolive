# Product Brief — AstroLive Compass

## One-line concept

AstroLive Compass turns astrology from passive prediction into an action-oriented daily planning layer: users check what kind of day they are having, plan meaningful moments, invite others into shared moments, and escalate complex decisions to AstroLive astrologers.

## Problem

Astrology products often become transactional or content-heavy: read a horoscope, generate a chart, or contact an astrologer when something goes wrong. That produces weak habitual usage and makes sharing feel bolted on rather than native.

AstroLive already offers many astrology utilities and consultation surfaces. The prototype should therefore add a behavioral layer rather than duplicate existing features.

## Product thesis

Astrology becomes more useful when it answers three practical questions:

1. What should I pay attention to today?
2. When is a good time to do something important?
3. How does this moment change when another person is involved?

The product loop is:

`Check today → Plan a moment → Act/share → Return tomorrow`

A second business loop is:

`High-intent moment → uncertainty/complexity → expert consultation`

## Final prototype scope

### Core screens

1. Landing / judge explainer
2. Lightweight onboarding
3. Today / Compass
4. Plan a Moment
5. Shared Moment
6. Experts handoff

### Optional only if core is finished

- Profile / saved moments
- History
- richer timeline interactions
- multiple astrology systems
- real account creation

## Primary jobs to be done

### Daily user

"Help me understand the tone of my day quickly enough that I can act on it."

### Planner

"Help me choose a better window for something important without making me decode astrology terminology."

### Couple/friend/team

"Help us understand a shared moment together and give us something worth sharing."

### High-intent user

"If this decision matters enough, let me talk to a real expert without starting over."

## Why this fits AstroHack

The concept intentionally addresses multiple challenge directions at once:

- Habit / retention: Today gives users a recurring check-in.
- Structural virality: Shared Moments create a natural invitation loop.
- Revenue: important moments can convert into astrologer consultation.
- Differentiated USP: action-first astrology is more distinct than another horoscope or Kundli feature.

## Non-goals

The hackathon prototype will not attempt to become a production astrology engine.

Out of scope:

- real astronomical calculation
- production Kundli generation
- payments
- actual consultation/video infrastructure
- complete authentication system
- admin panel
- CMS
- production notification system
- full multilingual rollout
- medical, legal, financial, or deterministic life-outcome claims

## Experience principles

1. Plain language before astrology jargon.
2. Action before explanation.
3. One useful recommendation per card is better than a wall of text.
4. Users should understand why a recommendation exists.
5. Shared experiences must be useful even to the invited person.
6. Astrology guidance is framed as reflective guidance, not certainty.
7. Human experts are an escalation path, not an aggressive upsell.

## Prototype success condition

A judge should be able to complete the following in under five minutes:

1. Understand the concept from the landing page.
2. Complete onboarding.
3. See a personalized Today state.
4. Create a planned moment.
5. Generate/open a shared moment.
6. Reach an expert handoff relevant to that moment.

If that journey works clearly, the prototype has done its job.
