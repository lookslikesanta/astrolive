## Internal hypotheses — not external facts

The following are product hypotheses and must be labeled as such in the report:

- Today/Compass may improve repeat engagement.
- Plan a Moment may increase actionable utility.
- Shared Moments may improve organic invites and invited-user activation.
- contextual expert handoff may improve consultation intent/conversion.

No prototype analytics exist yet to prove those outcomes.
